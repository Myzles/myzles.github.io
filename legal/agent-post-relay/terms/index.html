Agent Post Relay terms of service

Agent Post Relay, a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) app. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who may use it

Agent Post Relay is provided for communities moderated by its developer. Moderators who install it choose whether it posts automatically and can turn it off at any time in the app's community settings.

Content

Posts published by the app come from the developer's queue file. They must follow Reddit's User Agreement, Reddit Rules and each community's rules. Replies come from the same queue and are only made inside the community where the app is installed. The app does not post or comment in communities where it is not installed, and does not send messages or vote. Read requests only fetch public content, without usernames, for the developer's non-commercial use.
