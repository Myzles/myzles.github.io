Contact scrapers on Apify: per-page vs per-result pricing

If you search the Apify Store for a contact or email scraper, the most popular result is Contact Details Scraper (vdrmota/contact-info-scraper). It's well established, with about 4,000 monthly users, and it does the job. Its pricing is per page crawled, plus a start fee. That's worth understanding before you run it on a big list, because a business website is rarely one page.

I built Website Contact Finder with a different model: one flat price per website, charged only when contacts are found. Here's how the two compare in practice.

The math on 1,000 business websites

To find contacts, a scraper usually needs the homepage plus the contact page, and often the about or footer-linked pages too. Say that's 3 pages per site on average, and that 7 or 8 in 10 of the sites actually have contact details.

When per-page is cheaper

Be fair to the per-page model: if you only crawl one page per site, $0.002 per page beats $0.004 per site. And Website Contact Finder isn't the cheapest per-site option either. poidata/contact-details-scraper charges $0.002 per site. Pick on what you need, not on price alone.

FAQ

What's the difference between per-page and per-result pricing?

Per-page pricing charges for every page the crawler opens, whether or not it finds anything. Per-result pricing charges only for an outcome, here a website where contacts were found.

Is Website Contact Finder the cheapest contact scraper on Apify?

No. Some actors charge $0.002 per site, and a per-page scraper limited to one page per site is cheaper too. It's priced so you never pay for empty or dead sites.

Does it find more emails than Contact Details Scraper?

I don't know. I didn't run the paid competitors. Run both on a sample of your own list to see.

What happens if my run times out?

It stops a little before the limit, writes a row for every site it didn't reach marked Not checked, and doesn't charge for those.

Can I cap my spend?

Yes. Apify's max-spend setting stops the run cleanly when you reach it.
