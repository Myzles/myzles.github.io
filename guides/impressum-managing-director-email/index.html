How to find the managing director (Geschäftsführer) of a German company from its Impressum

If you sell to companies in Germany, Austria or Switzerland, there's a shortcut most prospecting tools ignore: the law requires most business websites to publish an Impressum (legal notice), and for a GmbH or AG it names the people who legally run the company. That's your decision maker, published by the company itself.

What the Impressum tells you

Line in the ImpressumMeaning
Geschäftsführer / GeschäftsführerinManaging director of a GmbH: the person who runs the company
Vorstand / VorstandsvorsitzenderManagement board (and its chair) of an AG
Inhaber / InhaberinOwner of a sole proprietorship (e.K. or freelancer)
Vertreten durch"Represented by": followed by the people above
Handelsregister, HRB numberCommercial register entry, useful to check the company exists
E-MailUsually a general address (info@, kontakt@), sometimes personal

Austria (Impressum or "Offenlegung") and Switzerland follow similar rules. Elsewhere in the EU you'll often find the same details under "Legal notice" or "Mentions légales".

From name to email

  1. Look for the format on the site. Any personal address (in the team page, a press contact or a PDF) tells you whether they use vorname.nachname@, v.nachname@ or vorname@.
  2. Handle umlauts. Müller usually becomes mueller@ (sometimes muller@). Try the "ue" form first.
  3. Verify, then label. Many smaller companies' domains accept all mail, so a verifier can't confirm the mailbox. Keep guessed addresses marked as guesses.
  4. Write in German if you can. And address them formally: "Sehr geehrter Herr Müller".

Germany's rules on B2B cold email are stricter than in many countries (unsolicited advertising emails generally need prior consent). Many teams use the Impressum for phone or letter outreach instead. Check what applies to you.

For a list of companies

By hand it's a minute per company: open the site, scroll to the footer, click Impressum, copy the name. For hundreds, use a tool that reads the Impressum and team pages for you, and keeps the source page so you can check.

FAQ

What is an Impressum?

A legal notice that most German, Austrian and Swiss business websites must publish. It names the company, its address, its register entry and the people who represent it.

Who is the decision maker in a German GmbH?

The Geschäftsführer (managing director), named in the Impressum. In an AG it is the Vorstand (management board).

Does the Impressum include an email address?

Usually a general one such as info@ or kontakt@. The managing director’s personal address has to be found or guessed from the company email format.

Can I cold email German companies?

German law is strict: unsolicited advertising emails generally need prior consent, even B2B. Many sellers use the Impressum for calls or letters instead. Get advice for your case.

How do umlauts appear in German email addresses?

Usually spelled out: ü becomes ue, ö becomes oe, ä becomes ae, ß becomes ss. So Müller is normally mueller@.
