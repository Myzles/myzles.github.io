How to find duplicate contacts in HubSpot

Duplicates creep in from form fills, list imports, sales reps adding people by hand, and integrations that don't check first. HubSpot blocks a second contact with the exact same email, but it won't stop "Bob" and "Robert" at the same company, or the same person with a work and a personal address.

Here are three ways to find them, depending on your plan and how many you have.

1. HubSpot's built-in duplicate tool

On plans that include it, go to Contacts, open Actions and choose Manage duplicates. HubSpot lists pairs it thinks match. You open each one, pick the record to keep and merge. Bulk merge and custom rules need Data Hub Professional.

Two things to watch. For contacts, the list can be long and noisy, so check pairs before merging. In a test I ran, under half the suggested contact pairs were real duplicates, though the highest-scored ones were much better. And merges can't be undone.

2. Export and check in a spreadsheet

This works on any plan and costs nothing, but it's slow.

  1. Export your contacts with first name, last name, email, phone and company.
  2. Add a helper column with a cleaned phone (digits only) and another with the email domain.
  3. Sort by last name, then by company, and scan for neighbours that look alike. Use COUNTIF on the cleaned phone and on first-plus-last name to flag repeats.
  4. For each real duplicate, open one of the records in HubSpot, click Actions, then Merge, and pick the other record.

Fine for 20 duplicates. Painful for 400, and exact-match formulas miss nicknames and typos.

If you're on Free or Starter, or the built-in list is too noisy, an app can do the matching and merging for you. I built Dupe Sweep for this. Keeping duplicates from coming back

  • Make email required on your forms, so HubSpot's own email check can do its job.
  • Before importing a list, match it against existing contacts on email and update instead of creating.
  • Check what your integrations do when they see a new email for a known person.
  • Run a scan monthly. Ten minutes a month beats a weekend cleanup once a year.

FAQ

Does HubSpot automatically remove duplicate contacts?

It prevents a new contact with the exact same email as an existing one. It doesn't merge contacts with different emails, nicknames or typos on its own.

How do I merge two contacts in HubSpot?

Open one contact, click Actions, choose Merge, search for the other contact, then pick which one to keep as primary. This works on every plan.

Can I bulk merge duplicates in HubSpot Starter?

Not with HubSpot's own tools. Bulk merging is part of Data Hub Professional. Apps such as Dupe Sweep add it on Free and Starter.

Can I undo a merge in HubSpot?

No. Check each pair first, and keep a record of the merged-away values. Dupe Sweep keeps this log for you.

How do I find duplicate companies in HubSpot?

The same three methods work. Companies often duplicate on name variants like Inc or LLC and on www versus non-www domains, so normalize those first if you use a spreadsheet.
