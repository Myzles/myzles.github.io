Why HubSpot duplicates keep coming back after a cleanup (and how to find the source)

A common story in HubSpot admin threads: someone spends a week merging duplicates, the database looks clean, and three months later it's just as bad. The merge wasn't wrong. Something upstream is still creating new records, and until you find it, every cleanup is temporary.

This guide shows how to find which source is making the duplicates, using properties every HubSpot account already has, and what to change once you know.

Step 1: Collect a sample of fresh duplicates

Don't start with the old mess. Look only at duplicates created after your last cleanup, because those point at the source that's still running.

  1. Make a contact view filtered to Create date is after your last cleanup.
  2. Add the columns Email, Company name, Record source, Record source detail 1, Original traffic source and Create date.
  3. Sort by last name, then by company, and note 15 to 20 pairs that are clearly the same person.

Property names change from time to time, so if one isn't there, search the column picker for "source".

Step 2: Read the source on the newer record of each pair

For each pair, the older record is usually fine. The newer one tells you what created the duplicate. Tally them up:

Newer record's source looks likeLikely causeFix
ImportRows with no email or a different email, so HubSpot couldn't match themAlways map Email, keep Record ID for re-imports, test 20 rows first
Form, same person with a new emailSomeone switched from a work to a personal address, or the reverseAsk for a work email; scan regularly for same name plus same company
Integration (Zapier, Make, webinar or ad lead sync)The step uses "create contact" instead of "find or create"Switch the action to search by email first, then create only if nothing is found
CRM or app syncTwo systems both creating records, each with slightly different dataChoose one system to create records and set the other to update only
Sales rep or manual entryContact created without searching first, or with a typo in the emailSearch first habit; HubSpot warns on an exact email match only
Company records, created automaticallyCompany domains in different formats, or a business with two domainsOne source of truth for companies; bare domains everywhere

In most accounts one or two sources cause nearly all of the duplicates. Fix those first.

Step 3: Fix the source, then clean once more

  • Integrations: open each zap or scenario that touches HubSpot contacts and check the action name. "Create or update" and "find contact, then create" are fine. Plain "create" is the usual culprit.
  • Imports: write a one-paragraph import rule for your team: email column mapped, emails trimmed, Record ID kept on re-imports. See how to stop imports creating duplicates.
  • Forms: make email required and avoid hidden fields that overwrite email.
  • Different emails, same person: HubSpot's own matching keys on email, so these never match automatically. You need a name plus company or phone check. See finding duplicates with different emails.

Only after the sources are fixed is it worth doing a full merge pass. Then keep a short monthly check of contacts created in the last 30 days, so anything new is caught before it spreads into lists and reports.

FAQ

Why do HubSpot duplicates come back after I merge them?

Because merging fixes records, not sources. An import, form or integration that creates new contacts without matching on email will keep making duplicates until it is changed.

How do I see what created a HubSpot contact?

Add the Record source and Record source detail properties as columns in a contact view. They show whether a record came from an import, form, integration, sales rep or app.

Do Zapier and Make create duplicate HubSpot contacts?

They can, when the step uses a plain create action. Use a find-or-create or create-or-update action that searches by email first.

Does HubSpot merge contacts with different emails automatically?

No. HubSpot matches on email address, so the same person with two addresses stays as two contacts until someone finds and merges them.

How often should I check for new duplicates?

Monthly is enough for most teams: look at contacts created in the last 30 days and merge the real pairs before they reach lists and reports.
