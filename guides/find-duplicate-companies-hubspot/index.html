How to find duplicate companies in HubSpot

Duplicate companies are sneakier than duplicate contacts. HubSpot matches companies on domain, so "acme.com" and "www.acme.com" can look like two businesses, and "Acme Inc" with no domain can sit next to "Acme" forever. Duplicate companies split deals, contacts and reporting, which is worse than a doubled email.

Here's how to find them by hand, what HubSpot catches on its own, and what it tends to miss.

The usual causes

Domain variants

The same company stored as acme.com, www.acme.com, https://acme.com/, acme.com/contact or ACME.com. These usually come from imports, enrichment tools and integrations that paste in a full website URL instead of the bare domain.

Name suffixes and "The"

"Acme", "Acme Inc", "Acme, Inc.", "Acme LLC" and "The Acme Company". When a record has no domain at all, the name is the only clue, and exact name matching misses all of these.

More than one real domain

acme.com and acme.co.uk, or a company that rebranded and kept its old domain. These may be duplicates, or deliberately separate regional records. Only someone who knows the business can say.

Parent and child companies

A head office and its branches can share a name and even a domain. HubSpot lets you link them as parent and child companies. Those aren't duplicates, and merging them loses the structure, so check before you merge.

Find them by hand (any plan)

  1. Go to Companies and export with name, company domain name, website URL, city, country, owner and number of associated contacts.
  2. In a spreadsheet, add a clean domain column: lower-case, remove https://, http:// and www., and cut everything after the first /.
  3. Add a clean name column: lower-case, remove punctuation, a leading "the", and trailing words like inc, llc, ltd, co, corp, company, gmbh.
  4. Use COUNTIF on each clean column to flag repeats, then sort by clean name and scan the neighbours.
  5. Also filter for companies with no domain. That group holds most of the hidden duplicates.
  6. For each real duplicate, open the company you want to keep in HubSpot, click Actions, then Merge, and pick the other one. Export the record you're merging away first. Merges can't be undone.

The same city and country help separate a real duplicate from two franchises with the same name.

What HubSpot's own tools catch and miss

HubSpot already prevents a lot of company duplicates: imports and automatic company creation match on the company domain name, so the exact same domain usually updates the existing company instead of creating a new one. On plans that include its duplicate management screen, it also suggests company pairs to review.

Where it falls short is anything that isn't close to an exact match. In my testing, HubSpot's company suggestions were accurate when they appeared, but they missed a good share of duplicates with typos, "The" or suffix changes, or domain formatting differences. Records with no domain are the hardest case for any domain-based check. Bulk merge and custom rules need Data Hub Professional.

Stop new duplicates

  • Store domains in one bare format (acme.com) in every import and integration.
  • Don't import companies without a domain unless you have to.
  • Decide whether companies come from automatic creation or from imports, not both at full speed.
  • Use parent and child links for branches instead of creating near-identical records.

FAQ

How do I find duplicate companies in HubSpot?

Export companies, clean the domain (remove www, https and paths) and the name (remove Inc, LLC and The), then flag repeats. Or use HubSpot's duplicate tool on plans that include it.

Does HubSpot deduplicate companies by domain?

Yes. Imports and automatic company creation match on the company domain name, so the same domain updates the existing company.

Why do I have duplicate companies with the same name?

Usually one record has no domain or a domain in a different format, so HubSpot couldn't match it to the existing company.

Should I merge parent and child companies?

No. Branches linked as child companies are separate on purpose. Merging them loses the structure.

Can I undo a company merge in HubSpot?

No. Export the company you're merging away before you merge.
