How to set up random coffee chats in Slack

Random coffee chats are the easiest way to get people on a remote or growing team talking to someone outside their own group. You can run them in Slack by hand for free. It takes about 15 minutes a round. Here's the full setup, and when an app is worth it.

1. Make an opt-in channel

Create a channel such as #coffee-chats. Post what it's for and how often pairs will go out (every week or every two weeks works for most teams). Only pair people who join the channel. Forced fun goes badly.

2. Shuffle the members

  1. Open the channel, click the member count, and copy the names into a spreadsheet (one per row, in column A).
  2. In column B put =RAND() and fill it down.
  3. Sort the sheet by column B. Every sort gives a new random order.
  4. Pair row 1 with row 2, row 3 with row 4, and so on.

3. Handle odd numbers and repeats

  • Odd count: put the last person into the final pair to make a group of three. Don't leave anyone out.
  • Repeats: keep a "History" tab with every pair you've sent and the date. Before sending, check each new pair against it and swap people between pairs if two have met recently.
  • Away or busy: let people react with an emoji to skip a round, and drop them from that week's sheet.

The repeat check is what makes doing this by hand get slow. With 10 people you'll run out of new pairs in about nine rounds. With 50 it's easy to miss a repeat by eye.

4. Send the intros

For each pair, start a group DM with both people (in Slack, new message, add both names) and send something like:

Hi both, you're this week's coffee chat pair. Find 20 minutes
before Friday, on a call or in person.
Starter question: what's something you learned recently
that wasn't for work?

Post a short "pairs are out" message in the channel so people know to check their DMs.

5. Repeat on a schedule

Set yourself a reminder so it doesn't lapse:

/remind me "Send coffee chat pairs" every other Monday at 9am

Slack's Workflow Builder can post a scheduled message to the channel, but it won't shuffle people into random pairs for you, so the sheet step stays manual.

When to use an app

Once you have more than about 20 people, or you keep forgetting, an app does the shuffle, the group DMs, the repeat check and the reminders. Donut is the best known; its free plan covers 24 people in one channel and paid plans start from $74 a month.

FAQ

Can Slack pair people randomly on its own?

No. Slack has no built-in random pairing, and Workflow Builder can't shuffle members. You need a spreadsheet and a manual step, or an app.

How often should coffee chats happen?

Every one or two weeks is common. Weekly suits small remote teams; every two weeks is easier for busy or larger teams.

What do you do with an odd number of people?

Make one group of three, so nobody is left out that round.

Is there a free Slack coffee chat app?

Donut's free plan covers 24 people in one channel. Team Connection is free during its beta.
