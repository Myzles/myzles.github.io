How to build a list of law firm partners and their emails

Law firms are one of the easiest professions to prospect, because almost every firm publishes its people. Partner pages usually list a name, a title, a practice area, and often a direct email and phone. The hard part is doing it for more than a handful of firms.

Where firms list their attorneys

PageUsually calledWhat's on it
People directoryAttorneys, Our People, Professionals, Lawyers, TeamEvery lawyer with title and office; large firms add filters
Bio pageOne page per attorneyDirect email, phone, bar admissions, practice areas, vCard
About / FirmAbout us, Our firmFounding or managing partners at small firms
Imprint (EU)Impressum, Legal notice, Mentions légalesPartners or managing directors named by law

Titles to look for when you want decision makers: managing partner, founding partner, name partner, senior partner, partner, and at in-house teams, general counsel. "Associate", "of counsel" and "paralegal" are usually not the buyer.

By hand, for a few firms

  1. Open the firm's site and find the attorneys page (check the main menu and the footer).
  2. Filter or scan for partners. Open each bio for the direct email.
  3. If the bio has a contact form instead of an email, look at any other attorney's address to learn the format (often first.last@ or the first initial plus last name@) and keep it marked as a guess.
  4. Note the practice area, because "partner, employment law" and "partner, M&A" are different buyers.

For hundreds of firms

  1. Get firm websites from a directory, a bar association listing or a Google Maps search like "law firm" in your target cities.
  2. Run the websites through a tool that reads each firm's own people and bio pages.
  3. Keep listed emails and guessed emails in separate columns, and verify guesses before you send.
  4. Check the email rules that apply to you. Lawyers are quick to notice careless outreach.

FAQ

Where can I find a list of law firm partners?

On each firm's own website: the attorneys or people directory and the individual bio pages. Bar association directories and Google Maps help you build the list of firms first.

Do law firms publish attorney email addresses?

Many do, on each attorney bio page. Some use contact forms instead; then the firm email format from another attorney is a good guess to verify.

Which titles are decision makers at a law firm?

Managing partner, founding or name partner, and senior partners, plus the office manager or COO for operations purchases. Associates rarely buy.

Is it legal to email lawyers whose addresses are public?

It depends on your country and theirs. Many places allow B2B email with an easy opt-out; some EU countries are stricter. Check before you send.

Can I scrape LinkedIn for law firm partners instead?

LinkedIn's terms forbid scraping. The firm's own website is public, usually more current, and more likely to have a direct email.
